# QC Checklist App Logo & Branding

## Logo Design Overview

The QC Checklist app uses the **Barnard** company logo as its primary brand identity.

### Logo Design

The Barnard logo features:
- **"BA" Bold Typography** — Modern, clean lettering
- **Growth Arrow** — Symbolizes progress, improvement, and upward trajectory
- **People Figures** — Represents team collaboration and human-centered focus
- **Tagline** — "People building for People" - reflects mission of supporting construction teams

### Brand Colors

| Element | Color | Usage |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Primary | `#004E89` (Blue) | Primary app theme |
| Success | `#059669` (Green) | Quality checks, verified items |
| Alert | `#EA580C` (Orange) | Alerts, warnings |
| Background | White | Clean, professional appearance |

### Brand Voice

**Company:** Barnard - People building for People
**Mission:** Empower construction teams to maintain quality and safety standards in the field.
**Values:**
- Accessibility — Easy to use for all team members
- Reliability — Offline-first, always available
- Safety — Built-in alerts for high-severity issues
- Collaboration — Team communication and shared accountability
- People-First Approach — Supporting teams in the field
